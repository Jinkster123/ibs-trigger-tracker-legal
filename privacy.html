Privacy Policy for IBS Trigger Tracker

Last updated: 26 October 2026

IBS Trigger Tracker (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Palm International. Contact: pjennings@hotmail.co.uk.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password or sign-in identifier.
  • Health entries: the information you log, such as meals, symptoms, bowel movements, stress, sleep and notes. This is sensitive health data.
  • Analytics: how you use the app (screens viewed, features used), device type, operating system, app version, and crash reports.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and we never see your card details.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account
  • To store your entries and show you patterns and likely triggers
  • To manage your subscription and unlock Pro features
  • To improve and fix the app
  • To contact you about your account or support requests

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health entries for advertising.

3. Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the app you signed up for
  • Explicit consent: to process your health data. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure and improve it

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app: RevenueCat (subscription management), Apple and Google (payments and app distribution), our hosting and database provider (secure storage of your data), and our analytics provider. These providers process data on our instructions. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Storage and security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on secure servers. Where data is transferred outside the UK/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data and health entries within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something (such as purchase records).

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account in the app (Settings) or by emailing pjennings@hotmail.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

9. Not medical advice

IBS Trigger Tracker is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the date. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email.

11. Contact

Palm International, pjennings@hotmail.co.uk
