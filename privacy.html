Privacy Policy for IBS Trigger Tracker

Last updated: 27 October 2026

IBS Trigger Tracker (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Palm International. Contact: pjennings@hotmail.co.uk.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash). You can also use an optional guest mode without an email.
  • Health entries: what you log, such as meals and food names, portions, symptoms and severity, bowel movements (Bristol scale and urgency), stress, sleep (hours and quality) and notes. This is sensitive health data.
  • Meal photos: if you add a photo of a meal, we store it.
  • AI-generated content: insight summaries, FODMAP plans, scans and favourites created for you in the app.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status, linked to your account ID. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and we never see your card details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, device ID or push token. The app does not include analytics, advertising or tracking software.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account
  • To store your entries and show you patterns and likely triggers
  • To detect foods in meal photos and generate insights (see section 4)
  • To manage your subscription and unlock Pro features
  • To fix problems and to contact you about your account or support requests

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health entries for advertising.

3. Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the app you signed up for
  • Explicit consent: to process your health data. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, and they process it on our instructions:

  • Database and hosting: managed MongoDB Atlas database and managed cloud infrastructure, including cloud object storage for meal photos.
  • AI service: meal photos and summarised symptom statistics are sent to an AI service to detect foods and produce insights.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management, linked to your account ID.
  • Apple and Google: payments and app distribution.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Storage, security and transfers

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest (AES-256). Your data is stored in the United States, and may also be processed in India. Where data is transferred outside the UK/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data and health entries within 30 days, and the content of your meal photos is permanently erased (a blank placeholder file may remain in storage until it is cleared), except where the law requires us to keep something (such as purchase records).

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Delete account and data) or by emailing pjennings@hotmail.co.uk. See also our Delete my data page. You can export a Doctor Report PDF from the app, and you can email us to request a copy of your other data. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

9. Not medical advice

IBS Trigger Tracker is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the date. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email.

11. Contact

Palm International, pjennings@hotmail.co.uk
