Delete my data

This page explains how to delete your account and data from IBS Trigger Tracker, provided by Palm International.

Option 1: in the app

Open the app, go to Settings and choose Delete account and data. Confirm when asked.

Option 2: by email

Email pjennings@hotmail.co.uk with the subject Delete my IBS Trigger Tracker data, from the email address you used to register. We may ask you to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

Your account, email address and all logged entries (meals, symptoms, bowel movements, stress, sleep and notes), plus usage data linked to your account. The content of your meal photos is permanently erased; a blank placeholder file may remain in storage until it is cleared. We complete deletion within 30 days. We may keep limited purchase records where the law requires.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
